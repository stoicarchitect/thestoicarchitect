Medium bio, 100 words

Daniel Brown MSc is a UK-based author, leadership professional and founder of the F5IVE Framework, a men's personal development system built on five interdependent pillars: Faith, Family, Finance, Fitness and Freedom. He holds an MSc in Psychology and a BSc (Hons) in Artificial Intelligence. His book, Vision to Victory: Why Men Fail, Why They Succeed, and How to Master the Difference, was written from his own redundancy, separation and five-year rebuild, and is available on Amazon. He creates content as The Stoic Architect at @danielmrbrown.
