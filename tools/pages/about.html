Capable men don't fail for the reasons they think

By every external measure Daniel was succeeding. Leading teams, building businesses, studying, working harder than most. Then, within months, almost everything came apart at once.

What the rebuild taught him was what he had watched in capable men his whole career: the problem was never talent, intelligence or effort. It was structure.

A framework built under load, not in a boardroom

The five pillars weren't found through theory. They were found by having to rebuild all of them together, with no room to fix one while ignoring the rest.

The insight was simple and structural. The five aren't independent. Every man Daniel had seen succeed in a way that lasted was holding all five in alignment. Every man he'd seen fall short, despite real ability, was optimising one while the others collapsed. That isn't victory. It's imbalance with better PR.
