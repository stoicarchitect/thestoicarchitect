In plain English. Essential storage keeps the site working and remembers your choice. Analytics and the LinkedIn tag are switched off until you allow them, and you can switch them off again whenever you like.

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small files a website stores in your browser. Similar technologies, such as local storage, work the same way. The law treats them alike, so this page covers both.

5. Changing your mind

Use Cookie settings, at the foot of every page, to switch analytics or marketing on or off. If you switch something off, we remove the analytics cookies this site set and stop loading the tags. You can also clear cookies in your browser settings.
