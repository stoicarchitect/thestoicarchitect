Before you join

When does the course open?

Everything is built: all five modules are recorded, and the workbooks and toolkit are finished. Doors open very shortly. Founding members get one email the moment they do, with the launch price held for them.

How is it delivered?

Online, at your own pace. Watch on your phone, tablet or laptop, download the workbooks and toolkit, and keep lifetime access, including any future updates to the Finance Pillar.

Do I need to have read the book first?

No. The course stands on its own. If you have read it, parts will feel like reinforcement, which is a good thing, not a requirement.

Is this investment advice?

No. The course is behavioural and structural: awareness, systems, alignment and identity. It won't tell you what to buy. Speak to a qualified adviser for that.

Does it work if I keep my finances Sharia-compliant?

That is how the course is meant to be used. Where an exercise touches debt, saving or investing, it can be completed without interest-based products, and investment examples include Sharia-screened options.

What if I don't finish it?

The 30-day guarantee covers you. But the modules are short on purpose, and the workbook is the actual mechanism of change. Skipping it is the one way to get less out of this than you paid for.
