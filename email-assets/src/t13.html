II

Family

“You’re not listening.”

 

{% if contact.FIRSTNAME %}{{ contact.FIRSTNAME }},{% else %}Brother,{% endif %}

My daughter tugged at my sleeve while I was half with her, half in my inbox, and said three words that landed harder than any performance review: ‘You’re not listening.’

She was not angry. She was honest. And I realised I was giving the best of myself to strangers and the leftovers to the people who mattered most.

This is the pillar men get backwards. We are told a man's job is to provide. Provide you will. That is not the metric.

The metric is presence.

Your children will forget what you earned. They will not forget whether you were there. Presence is harder than provision because it cannot be outsourced. You have to show up yourself.

Today's action

Open your calendar for this week. Find one block where you are fully present with the people who matter. No phone. No mental escape. Protect it like your life depends on it.
