III

Finance

Most men earn. Very few build.

 

{% if contact.FIRSTNAME %}{{ contact.FIRSTNAME }},{% else %}Brother,{% endif %}

After the redundancy I opened my banking app every morning. Nothing had changed overnight. I just needed to check, the way you press a bruise to see if it still hurts.

Most men know how to earn money. Far fewer know how to build it.

Earning is linear. More hours, more income, until you burn out. Building is architectural. You construct a structure that keeps working when you stop.

The order matters: income, expenses, savings, investment. Stabilise what comes in. Control what goes out, not because you are cheap, but because the gap between the two is your power. That gap becomes savings. Savings becomes investment.

For me there is one more filter. As a Muslim, building has to be halal. No riba. Interest-bearing debt is the first thing cleared, and every investment has to pass a Sharia test before it gets my money. The structure is the same. The filter just makes it cleaner.

Today's action

Pull your last three months of statements. Find three expenses you do not need and cut them. Do not get elaborate. Three. That is your first move from earning to building.
